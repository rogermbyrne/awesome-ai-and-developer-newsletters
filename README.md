## How to pick a newsletter to sponsor

- Start with the reader. Pick newsletters whose readers could use your product this month, and only then compare audience size.
- Read the last four issues. Check that it still goes out on time, how many sponsors each issue carries, and whether those sponsors sell to the same people you do.
- Ask the publisher for open and click numbers from recent sends, and for a past sponsor in your category.
- Test one send before you book a run. It won't tell you everything, but you'll see whether readers click.
- Send the clicks to a page written for that audience, with the trial or offer at the top, not your homepage.
- Write down what counts as a win, such as signups, trials or booked demos, before you book.
